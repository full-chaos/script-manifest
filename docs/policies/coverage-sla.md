# Coverage SLA Policy

Coverage providers must publish turnaround windows before checkout, acknowledge new orders within one business day, and deliver coverage notes by the committed due date.

If an order misses its committed delivery window without buyer-approved extension, Script Manifest support may escalate the order, request a revised delivery plan, or apply the dispute and refund workflow.

Repeated SLA misses can affect provider verification state, marketplace visibility, and continued eligibility to sell coverage services on Script Manifest.

Last updated: May 2026.
